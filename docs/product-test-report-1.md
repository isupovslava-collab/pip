# Product Test Report 1

## Scope

Sprint 9 establishes a measurable production baseline before further library expansion. The test surface includes the Premium Curated Core, honest low/zero coverage, Fresh Discovery v3, two-step AI handoff, wizard context retention, and feedback schema v6.

## Baseline decision

Only the six previously approved production Heroes are initially premium/eligible. Meeting and Strategy remain review-only. All other existing references remain physically present and explicitly excluded or unknown until a separate visual/product review. No result is promoted from legacy metadata alone.

## Review questions

- Did the selected primary slide type stay exact?
- Was each visible PIP result genuinely premium and usable as inspiration?
- Were zero/low coverage states understandable and honest?
- Did Fresh Discovery links open and lead to the claimed material?
- Were its sources and compositions diverse?
- Did users understand copy-first/provider-second handoff?
